Built different, on purpose

Orbit apps follow four rules, no exceptions. It's why families trust them and kids can use them safely.

🚫No ads — ever

No ad SDKs, no banners, no "watch this to continue." The apps make money from subscriptions, not your attention.

🔒No tracking

No data brokers, no selling your info, no creepy analytics. Your family's data stays your family's.

🧒Kid-safe by design

Built to be safe for kids from the first line of code — including a dedicated Kid Mode in Cooking Dojo.

🥤100% alcohol-free

Every recipe and lesson is family-friendly. The Drinks path is coffee, tea, smoothies and zero-proof mocktails.
