Questions

Before you visit.

Do I need to book ahead?+
Same-week appointments are usually available, but weekends fill fast. Call (403) 555-0117 to reserve your time.
What should I expect on my first visit?+
Arrive 10 minutes early for tea and a short consultation. Your therapist will ask about pressure, problem areas and anything to avoid.
Are your massage therapists registered?+
Yes — all massage therapists are registered, so receipts are available for insurance claims.
What are your hours?+
Tuesday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM. Sunday and Monday by appointment for packages.
Do you sell gift cards?+
Yes — gift cards for any amount or treatment, available in person or by phone.
Where are you located?+
418 8 Ave SE, Calgary — street parking nearby, and we validate for the parkade across the street.
