It depends on the vehicle, its condition and the service you choose — a full body wash is much quicker than a complete detail inside and out. Give us a call and we'll give you a realistic time for your car.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is the best way to make sure we can fit you in when it suits you. Call us and we'll find a time.

Yes. Pet hair removal is part of a full interior detail. Mention it when you book so we set aside enough time.
