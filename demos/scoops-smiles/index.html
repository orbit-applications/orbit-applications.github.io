FAQ

Sweet answers.

Do you have dairy free options?+
Yes — always at least four sorbets and two oat milk scoops in the case. Mango Tango is the legend.
Can I order an ice cream cake?+
Yes — any two flavours, with a fudge crunch layer. 48 hours notice, from $38. Call (403) 555-0123.
Do you cater events?+
Sundae bar catering from $12/person within Calgary. Two weeks notice is ideal in summer.
Are dogs allowed?+
Pup cups are free, always. Dogs welcome on the patio.
What are your hours?+
Summer (May–Sep): daily 11 AM–10 PM. Winter: Tue–Sun 12–8 PM, closed Mondays.
Where are you located?+
334 19 St NW, Calgary — two blocks from the river pathway.
