About

A neighbour first, an agent second.

Foothills Homes was started by Tom Beckett, who has lived, worked and raised his kids in the foothills for over twenty years. He sells homes the way he'd want his own family to be treated — straight talk, no pressure, and answers in plain English.

Whether you're buying your first place or selling the family home, you get one person from the first call to moving day.

I'm a first-time buyer. Where do I start?

Start with a conversation. Call (403) 555-0149 and we'll talk budget, neighbourhoods and next steps — including how pre-approvals work. No obligation, no jargon.

What's my home worth?

Online estimates are rough guesses. We do free, no-pressure valuations based on real recent sales in your neighbourhood. Call (403) 555-0149 to set one up.

Should I buy first or sell first?

It depends on your timeline, your equity and the market. We'll walk through the options honestly so you can choose what fits your life.

Do you work outside Calgary?

Yes — Cochrane, Okotoks, Springbank and the surrounding foothills are our home turf. Ask about any community when you call.

How do showings work?

Call or text (403) 555-0149 with the home you're interested in and we'll arrange a private showing, usually within a day or two.

What are your fees?

Commission is discussed openly up front — no surprises, no fine print. Call for a straightforward breakdown.
