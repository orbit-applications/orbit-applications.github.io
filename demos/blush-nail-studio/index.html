How long does gel polish last?

About 2–3 weeks with normal wear. We'll also tell you how to keep them glossy between visits.

Do I need an appointment?

Guests without an appointment are welcome when a chair is free, but calling (403) 555-0124 ahead guarantees your spot — especially on weekends.

What are your hours?

Tuesday–Saturday 10 AM – 7 PM, Sunday 11 AM – 5 PM. Closed Mondays.

Can you do nail art from a photo?

Yes — bring the photo or send a description when you call, and we'll tell you what's possible in your appointment time.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes! Bridal sets plus trials for the wedding party. Call for package pricing.
