FAQ

Can I reserve bread ahead?

Yes — call (403) 555-0132 by 6 PM the day before and we'll set loaves aside with your name on them.

How far ahead for custom cakes?

1–2 weeks for celebration cakes, 4+ weeks for weddings. Last minute? Call — we sometimes have case cakes ready to customize.

Do you do gluten free or vegan?

We bake a small daily selection of both — call in the morning to hear what's on the shelf today.

Do you wholesale to cafés?

Yes! We supply several local cafés with daily bread and pastries. Call to talk wholesale.
