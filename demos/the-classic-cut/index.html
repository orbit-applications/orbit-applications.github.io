Our Story

One barber. One shop.
About sixty years.

The same chair has served downtown Calgary for six decades — long enough to have cut the hair of generations of the city: the fathers, then the sons, then the grandsons.

The shop keeps the traditions that made it a downtown fixture: a real hot towel shave, a shoe shine by the chair, and the kind of conversation you only get in a barbershop that has heard it all.

You'll find us at 717 7 Ave SW, weekdays from 9 to 5.

FAQ

How much is a cut?

Pricing depends on the service and your hair. Call (403) 555-0110 for a quote.

Do I need to book ahead?

Walk in during shop hours, weekdays 9 to 5. Calling ahead is the best way to make sure a chair is ready for you.

Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes. The traditional straight razor shave with hot towels is one of the shop's specialties.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Ask when you call. Mention your child's age and the cut they would like.
