Our Story

One barber. One shop.
About sixty years.

Barber Joe Astorino has been cutting hair at Elveden House for roughly sixty years — long enough to have cut the hair of generations of downtown Calgary: the fathers, then the sons, then the grandsons.

The shop keeps the traditions that made it a downtown fixture: a real hot towel shave, a shoe shine by the chair, and the kind of conversation you only get in a barbershop that has heard it all.

You'll find us inside Elveden House at 717 7 Ave SW, weekdays from 9 to 5.
