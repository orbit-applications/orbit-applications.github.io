FAQ

Before you sign a lease.

What does space actually cost?

It depends on the area, the building and the term. Call (403) 555-0115 with your size and budget and we will give you a straight range before you tour anything.

How long does a lease take?

Most deals close in 4–8 weeks from first tour to signed lease. Tight deadlines are common — tell us yours up front and we will plan around it.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes — from a 1,500 sq ft storefront to a 60,000 sq ft warehouse. Small tenants get the same attention as the big ones.

What is an off market listing?

Space the landlord has not advertised publicly. Much of our deal flow works this way — one call gets you access to it.

Do you charge tenants?

In most leases the landlord pays our fee, so tenant representation costs you nothing out of pocket. Ask us when you call.

Can you help with a renewal?

Yes. We review your current lease, check the market, and negotiate your renewal or a move.
