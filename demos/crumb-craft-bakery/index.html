Our story

Flour, water,
patience.

Crumb & Craft started in a home kitchen with one sourdough starter and a secondhand oven. Twelve years later the starter is still going — and so are we.

  • Everything baked in house, every morning — never par baked or frozen
  • Stone milled Alberta flour and cultured butter
  • Day old bread donated daily, never wasted

FAQ

Fresh answers.

Do I need to order ahead?+
For daily breads and pastries, just come in. For custom cakes, give us two weeks. For babka Fridays, call (403) 555-0120 to reserve.
Do you do wedding cakes?+
Yes — wedding cakes are a specialty. Book a tasting consultation at least two months out.
Anything gluten free?+
A small rotating selection of gluten free bakes, made first each morning to avoid cross contact. Ask what's in the case today.
Do you wholesale to cafés?+
We supply a handful of local cafés with bread and pastries. Call to talk wholesale.
Where are you located?+
8812 Macleod Trail S, Calgary — with parking right out front.
What are your hours?+
Tuesday to Friday 7 AM–5 PM, Saturday 8 AM–4 PM, Sunday 8 AM–2 PM. Closed Mondays.
