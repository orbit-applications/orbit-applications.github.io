What is a Russian manicure?

A meticulous dry manicure using an electric file for ultra clean cuticles and a seamless grow out. It takes longer — and lasts longer.

Can I come without an appointment?

Evenings book out fast, so we strongly recommend calling (403) 555-0125 ahead. Daytime visits without an appointment are sometimes possible.

How far ahead should I book bridal?

At least 4–6 weeks for the trial, so there's time to perfect the set before the day.

What are your hours?

Tuesday–Saturday 11 AM – 9 PM, Sunday 12 – 5 PM. Closed Monday.

Where are you?

812 1 St SE, Calgary, AB. Get directions.

Do you do fills on sets from other salons?

Yes, after a quick look at your current set. Call and we will tell you honestly whether a fill or a fresh set is better.
