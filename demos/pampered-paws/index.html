Good to Know

Questions, answered.

What if my dog is between sizes?

No problem — call us with your dog's breed and weight and we'll confirm which size rate applies before you book. Call (403) 555-0140.

My dog is nervous about grooming. Can you help?

Yes. The studio is cage free and one dog at a time, and we're certified in low stress handling. Nervous dogs get extra time, breaks and treats — never forced.

How far ahead should I book?

We usually book 1–2 weeks out. Call or text and we'll find the next spot that works for you.

Do you take walk ins for nail trims?

Yes — nail trims are C$18 and walk ins are welcome during studio hours, Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

How long does a full groom take?

It depends on your dog's size and coat. Ask when you book and we will give you a time to expect, then text you photos while they are with us.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes. Puppies get a short, gentle first groom so they learn the tub, the dryer and the brush are nothing to worry about. Call (403) 555-0140 to ask when to bring yours in.
