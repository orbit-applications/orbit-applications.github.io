Services

What we handle.

Standard handyman work for your home. Every job is quoted on its own — call with the details.

S-01

General Repairs

The things that break, stick, squeak or leak around the house. Think dented or holed drywall, doors and drawers that won't close properly, and railings or trim that have worked loose. When you call, describe what's wrong, where it is and roughly how big — the more detail, the clearer the quote.

  • Drywall patches
  • Sticking doors & drawers
  • Loose railings & trim
Call for a quote
S-02

Carpentry & Trim

Cutting, fitting and finishing for the wood parts of your home. That covers replacing or re-securing baseboards and door casing, putting up shelving, and small framing fixes. Mention the room, approximate lengths, and whether you already have the material on hand.

  • Baseboards & casing
  • Shelving
  • Small framing fixes
Call for a quote
S-03

Fixtures

Swapping out worn or outdated fixtures around the kitchen and bath. Faucets, shower heads, towel bars and fresh caulking or sealing around tubs and sinks. If you've already bought the replacement, have the make and model handy when you call.

  • Faucets & shower heads
  • Towel bars & accessories
  • Caulking & sealing
Call for a quote
S-04

Doors & Hardware

Getting doors to close right and hardware to work the way it should. Swapping locks and handles, tightening or replacing hinges, and adjusting strikes so the latch catches. Tell us how many doors, whether they're interior or exterior, and what they're doing — sticking, swinging open or not latching.

  • Locks & handles
  • Hinges & strikes
  • Door adjustments
Call for a quote
S-05

Painting & Exterior

Touch-ups and upkeep, inside the house and out. Interior wall and trim touch-ups, plus small fence, deck, siding and trim repairs. Let us know the size of the area and whether you have matching paint or material when you call.

  • Interior touch-ups
  • Fence & deck repairs
  • Siding & trim fixes
Call for a quote
S-06

Seasonal Upkeep

Small jobs that help a house handle Calgary winters and chinooks. Weatherstripping doors, sealing drafty gaps, and fixing loose or leaking gutters and downspouts. Mention where you feel the draft or see the water, and call to ask about availability before the season turns.

  • Weatherstripping
  • Draft sealing
  • Gutter & downspout fixes
Call for a quote
