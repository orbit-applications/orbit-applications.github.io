How much does a set cost?
Shellac manicure from $35, pedicure with shellac $48, full set with shellac from $60. Call (403) 555-0114 for an exact quote on your service.
Do I need an appointment?
Guests without an appointment are welcome and usually seen right away. Calling ahead is the safer bet on weekends, when the salon is busiest.

Sunday 10 AM – 5 PM, Monday to Friday 10 AM – 7 PM, Saturday 10 AM – 6 PM.

Yes — kids’ nails are a regular request here. Mention it when you call so the right amount of time is set aside.

Yes — brows, face waxing and threading are all available, and easy to add to a manicure or pedicure visit.
