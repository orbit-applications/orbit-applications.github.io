How long do extensions last?

With fills every 2–3 weeks, a set can be maintained indefinitely. A lift + tint lasts 6–8 weeks on its own.

Will extensions damage my natural lashes?

Not when applied correctly — we match the weight to what your natural lashes can hold, and never overload them.

What's the difference between classic and volume?

Classic is one extension per natural lash (natural look). Volume uses handmade fans of 2 to 6 ultra fine lashes for fullness.

Can I wear makeup with lashes?

Yes — just avoid oil based and waterproof products around the eyes, and we'll show you how to cleanse them.
