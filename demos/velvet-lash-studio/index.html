FAQ

Got Q’s?

Quick answers below. For anything specific, just call.

How long do lash extensions last?
With fills every 2–3 weeks, a set can be maintained indefinitely. Without fills, expect 3–4 weeks of good wear.
Will extensions damage my natural lashes?
Not when applied correctly — every set is mapped to what your natural lashes can safely hold. Never pull extensions off yourself; book a $25 removal.
What’s the difference between classic and volume?
Classic is one extension per lash (natural, mascara like). Volume uses handmade fans of ultra fine lashes for a fuller, fluffier look. Mega volume goes further still.
How should I prepare for my appointment?
Arrive with clean, makeup free lashes and skip waterproof mascara for 48 hours before. Contact lenses should come out before application.
Do you do bridal parties?
Yes — bridal sets and party bookings are available. Call (403) 555-0116 to plan timing around the big day.
