Questions

Before your first session

I have never played. Where do I start?

Tuesday beginners night. Characters are pre made, dice are on the table, and the dungeon master explains every roll.

Can I bring my own character?

Yes. Any level one to five character fits our tables. Bring your sheet or your app.

Can I join a campaign that has already started?

Usually, yes. Thursday tables add new players between story arcs. Book a seat and we will match you to a table with space.

Can I run my own campaign here?

Yes. Book a table for your group and it is yours for the night, with maps and terrain to borrow.

Can I roll dice before buying them?

Yes. Every set on the dice wall has a tray next to it. Roll as many as you like.

What if a book is out of stock?

We order every week. Leave your name and number and we will call you when it arrives.
