Services

Barber chair & salon chair.

Whether it's a quick clean-up or a whole new look, here's the kind of work Harbour Cut does. Call for pricing and to check availability.

Barber

Haircuts

Classic and modern cuts with clippers and scissors — short, long or somewhere in between. Good for a regular clean-up or a change of style, and a photo of the look you want always helps. When you call, mention roughly how long it's been since your last cut and what you'd like done.

Call to book

Fades & Tapers

Clean blends, sharp edges and line-ups, for straight, wavy, curly and coily hair. Low, mid or high, down to the skin or longer on the sides — tell the barber how short you like it and where you want the fade to start. Ask about a quick line-up to freshen things between full cuts.

Call to book

Beard Trims

Shaping, trimming and tidy lines to keep a beard looking intentional. From a short stubble clean-up to shaping a fuller beard, the cheek and neck lines are set to suit your face. Pair it with a haircut so everything blends — just mention both when you book.

Call to book
Salon

Cut & Style

Salon cuts for any length — trims, layers, bobs and reshapes, finished with a blow-dry. A good fit whether you're growing your hair out, going shorter or just tidying the ends. Let the shop know your current length and what you're after when you call, so the right amount of time is set aside.

Call to book

Blowouts & Styling

A polished finish for a night out, an event or just because — smooth, volume or texture. Getting ready for something specific? Mention the date and time when you call. Bring a reference photo if you have a look in mind.

Call to book

Wash & Care

A relaxing shampoo and condition before your cut or style, so you start fresh in the chair. Mention it when you book if you'd like it added to your appointment. Ask about other salon services when you call.

FAQ

Quick answers.

For anything specific, the fastest answer is a phone call.

(403) 555-0109
Do you cut all hair types?

Harbour Cut is a barber shop and hair salon offering cuts for everyone. Tell the shop about your hair and what you're after when you call (403) 555-0109.

What are your hours?

Please call (403) 555-0109 for current hours and availability, or check @harbourcutbarber.

How much is a cut?

Pricing depends on the service and your hair. Call (403) 555-0109 for a quote.

Do I need to book ahead?

Calling ahead is the best way to make sure there's a chair ready for you. The shop can let you know what's open.

Do you do colour or other salon services?

Ask when you call — the shop can tell you which salon services are available and how to book them.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Kids' cuts are one of the services to ask about. Call (403) 555-0109 to check availability, and mention your child's age and the cut they'd like.

What should I mention when I call?

Let the shop know which service you're after — a barber cut, fade, beard trim, salon cut or styling — plus your hair type and roughly how long it is. If you're combining services, like a cut and beard trim, say so up front. A photo of the look you want helps too.
