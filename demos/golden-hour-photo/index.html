FAQ

Good questions.

How far ahead should we book?+
Family and portrait sessions: 2–4 weeks. Weddings: 6–12 months — 2027 dates are open now. Call (403) 555-0119 to check.
What happens if it rains?+
We watch the forecast together and reschedule free if the light won't cooperate. No fee, no stress.
How many photos do we get?+
Family: 40+. Portrait: 25+. Newborn: 35+. Wedding: 400+. Every image is hand-edited and delivered in a private online gallery.
Do we get printing rights?+
Yes — every session includes full printing rights, plus guidance on where to print for the best quality.
Can we bring our dog?+
Absolutely — dogs are family. Mention it when you call so we pick a dog-friendly spot.
Where do sessions happen?+
Outdoors around Calgary — Fish Creek, Nose Hill, the river pathways — or in your home for newborns.
