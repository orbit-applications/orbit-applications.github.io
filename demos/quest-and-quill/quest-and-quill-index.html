Questions

Something else on your mind? Call or text (403) 555-0135.

Do I need experience to join game night?

No. Game night starts with a staff member teaching the rules, and loaner games are free to borrow.

I am coming alone. Is that okay?

Very. Most people arrive solo. Tell us at the counter and we will seat you at a table that has room.

What do I bring to paint night?

Nothing. Brushes, paints, and your first miniature are included on your first night.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Family tables run on Friday nights and kids under twelve play free.

What if a game is out of stock?

We order every week. Leave your name and number and we will call you when it arrives.

Can I book a table for my group?

Yes. Use the booking form and pick a night. Tables are free with any purchase in store.
