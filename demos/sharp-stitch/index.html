Suits cut to your measurements and finished by hand — from C$895. Choose your fabric, lining and details with the tailor, then come in for fittings. When you call, mention when you need it and whether it is for a wedding or an event.

FAQ

Quick answers.

For anything specific, the fastest answer is a phone call.

(403) 555-0144
What do alterations cost?

Hemming from C$18, jacket work from C$35, suit alterations from C$45, made-to-measure suits from C$895. Call (403) 555-0144 for a quote on your garment.

What are your hours?

Please call (403) 555-0144 for current hours and availability, or check @sharpstitchcalgary.

How long do alterations take?

Simple hems are often back in a few days; suit work takes longer. Ask about turnaround when you call.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when the bench is free, but calling ahead means a fitting time is waiting for you.

Do you make custom suits?

Yes — made-to-measure suits from C$895, cut to your measurements with your choice of fabric and details. Call to book a fitting.

Do you do wedding tailoring?

Yes — grooms, groomsmen and guests, from C$120. Call early in wedding season, it books up fast.

What should I mention when I call?

The garment, what you want done, and when you need it. Bring the shoes and shirt you will wear with it to the fitting.
