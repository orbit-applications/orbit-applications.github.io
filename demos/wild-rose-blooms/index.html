FAQ

Before you order

Quick answers. Anything else — just call.

How fast is delivery?

Order by 1 PM for same day delivery in Calgary. Delivery is C$12 flat, free over C$75.

Can you make something custom?

Custom is our favourite word. Call (403) 555-0139 with your colours, budget and vibe and we will design something one of a kind.

How far ahead should we book a wedding?

Peak months (June–September) book 6+ months out. Wedding minimum is C$1,200 and consults are free.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes — weekly office or home flowers from C$40/week. Pause anytime, swap styles whenever you like.

Do you deliver outside Calgary?

We deliver across Calgary and nearby towns like Cochrane and Canmore. Call for a quote outside the city.

What if my flowers fade fast?

Tell us within 7 days and we will replace the bouquet or make it right.
