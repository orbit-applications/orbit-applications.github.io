The menu

Every pair
gets done.

Runners, retros, canvas, leather, suede, knit — every material needs a different approach. Tell us what you've got and what's bugging you about it, and we'll tell you what we can do.

KC—01

Deep Clean

Uppers, midsoles, outsoles and laces worked over in detail — dirt, grime and everyday stains lifted with material-safe products. It's the go-to for daily wearers, runners and pairs that have seen a few muddy walks. When you call, mention the brand or model, the materials and the spots that bother you most.

Call for a quote
KC—02

Sole Whitening

Yellowed, grey or oxidized midsoles and icy soles treated to bring back a brighter, cleaner look. Yellowing builds up with age and light exposure, and how far it comes back depends on the rubber and how long it's been happening. Describe the soles over the phone and ask what's realistic for your pair.

Call for a quote
KC—03

Suede, Nubuck & Knit

Delicate materials get a gentle touch — careful cleaning, nap restoration on suede and nubuck, and knit uppers handled with care. These materials stain and flatten easily, so they need a different method than leather or canvas. Tell us what the material is and what kind of mark you're dealing with — water spots, salt, dirt or something else.

Call for a quote
KC—04

Restoration

For pairs that need more than a clean — scuff and crease touch-ups, colour restoration and repaint work on worn areas. Best for older favourites, retros and pairs with faded or worn-through colour. Photos or a description of the damage help when you call, and we'll talk through what restoration can and can't do.

Call for a quote
KC—05

Laces & Insoles

Laces washed or swapped out, insoles cleaned and freshened — the small details that make a pair feel new again. Dingy laces and smelly insoles can make even a clean pair look and feel tired. Ask about lace swaps or insole freshening on their own or added to a clean.

Call for a quote
KC—06

Protection

A protective coating after cleaning to help your pairs resist water and stains — useful for a Calgary winter of slush and salt. It's a smart add-on right after a deep clean, especially for suede, nubuck and light-coloured pairs. Ask about it when you arrange your drop-off.

How much does a clean cost?
Pricing depends on the shoe, the material and what it needs. Call (403) 555-0113 for a quote.
Can I just walk in?
You'll find us at 956 Erin Woods Dr SE in Calgary. Please call ahead to arrange a drop-off time.
How long will it take?
It depends on the job and how busy we are. Ask about turnaround when you call and we'll give you an idea.
Can you get every stain out?
Some stains and wear lift right out; others can only be improved. Describe the damage over the phone and we'll be straight with you about what's realistic.
Do you clean shoes that aren't sneakers?
Call and tell us what you have — leather shoes, boots and other footwear are worth asking about.
Can I drop off more than one pair?
Multiple-pair drop-offs are worth asking about. When you call (403) 555-0113, list each pair and what it needs so we can talk through pricing and timing.
Can you deal with winter salt stains?
Salt stain removal is one of the things to ask about. Results depend on the material and how long the salt has been sitting, so describe the shoe and the staining when you call — and the sooner after a slushy day, the better.
