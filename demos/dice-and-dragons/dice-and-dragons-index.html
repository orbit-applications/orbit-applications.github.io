Questions from the counter

Can I roll dice before I buy them?

Yes. Every set in the vault is there to be rolled. Use the trays at the wall and find the set that feels right.

Do you buy single Magic cards?

Yes, for store credit. Bring your binder and we will price your cards with you at the counter.

I am new to Magic. Is Friday Night Magic for me?

Yes. Commander tables welcome new players and loaner decks are free to borrow for the night.

Which roleplaying games do you stock?

Dungeons and Dragons, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, and a rotating shelf of indie systems. Ask and we will order what we do not have.

How does Sunday dice trading work?

Bring the sets you no longer roll and swap them with other players at the table. Staff are around to help settle trades.

Can I book a table for my group?

Yes. Use the booking form and pick a night. Tables are free with any purchase in store.
