Alterations & repairs

Hemming

Jeans, trousers, skirts and dresses shortened to the length you want. For the best result, bring the shoes you plan to wear with the item, since heel height changes where a hem should sit. When you call, mention the fabric and whether it's a plain, lined or cuffed hem.

Call for a quote

Zipper Replacement

Stuck, split or broken zippers swapped out on jackets, pants, dresses and more. Let us know what kind it is — a separating jacket zip, a trouser fly or a hidden dress zip — and roughly how long. If only the pull has come off or the teeth won't close, mention that too and ask what the fix involves.

Call for a quote

Mending & Fit

Tears, holes and loose seams repaired; garments taken in or let out for a better fit. For fit changes, plan to try the piece on so it can be pinned where it needs adjusting. How much a garment can be let out depends on its seam allowance, so describe the item when you call.

Call for a quote

Laundry & cleaning

Laundry Service

Drop off your everyday laundry and pick it up clean — one less chore on your list. Point out any delicates, items that shouldn't go in the dryer, or stains you want us to know about. Call to ask about timing for your load.

Call to ask

Garment Cleaning

Cleaning for shirts, blouses, jackets and other pieces — call to ask what we can take. Check the care label before you come in and show us any spots or marks at drop-off. For wool, silk, beading or other special-care pieces, ask when you call.

Call to ask

Pressing

Shirts, trousers and dresses pressed crisp and ready to wear. Handy before an interview, a wedding or a trip, or as the finishing step after a hem or repair. If you need an item for a specific day, ask about timing when you call.

FAQ

Quick answers

Don't see your question? Just give us a call.

How much does an alteration or repair cost?
Pricing depends on the item and the work. Call (403) 555-0103 for a quote.
How fast can you get it done?
We aim for a quick turnaround, but timing depends on the job and how busy we are. Call to ask about timing for your item.
Can I bring laundry and alterations at the same time?
We offer both alterations and laundry/cleaning services. Call ahead and let us know what you're bringing.
Do I need to try the item on?
For fit changes like hemming or taking in, trying the garment on usually gives the best result. Call to ask what works best for your item.
Can you fix my zipper / tear / missing button?
Most everyday repairs are worth asking about. Describe it when you call and we'll let you know.
What should I bring for a hem or fit change?
Bring the garment itself, and for trousers, skirts or dresses, the shoes you'll wear with it. If you have a piece that already fits the way you like, it can help to bring that along as a reference. Call ahead to ask about timing.
