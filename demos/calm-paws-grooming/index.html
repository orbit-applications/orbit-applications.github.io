Services

Gentle care, coat to claw.

Every dog is groomed according to its coat, its comfort and what you'd like done. Call to talk through what your dog needs.

Full Groom

Bath, blow-dry, brush-out and haircut or trim, finished with a tidy of nails, ears and paws. Suited to poodles, doodles, shih tzus and other dogs whose coats keep growing and need regular cutting. When you call, mention your dog's breed, any matting, and the length or style you'd like.

Call to book

Bath & Brush

A thorough wash and dry with a careful brush-out — ideal between haircuts or for short-coated dogs. It's a good fit for dogs who don't need a haircut but could use a proper clean. Let us know about sensitive skin or any shampoo your vet has recommended, and if your dog is a heavy shedder, ask what the brush-out can cover.

Call to book

Trims & Tidy-Ups

Face, feet and sanitary trims to keep your dog neat and comfortable between full grooms. Handy when hair is growing into the eyes or collecting snow and grit between the paw pads. Tell us which areas you'd like done when you call.

Call to book

Nails & Paw Care

Nails trimmed and paw pads tidied, done slowly and patiently for dogs who aren't fans of having their feet handled. Overgrown nails can change how a dog stands and walks, so regular trims help keep them comfortable. Mention if your dog has dark nails or gets nervous about paw handling.

Call to book

Puppy Introductions

Short, low-pressure visits that let young dogs get used to water, brushes and clippers in a quiet space. Early, positive experiences can make future grooms easier for everyone. Tell us your puppy's age when you call, and ask what a first visit looks like.

Questions

Good to know.

Are you taking new clients?

Yes — Calm Paws is now accepting new clients. Call (403) 555-0106 to introduce your dog and find a time.

What does "one-on-one" mean?

Appointments are one-on-one, so your dog gets the groomer's full attention in a calm, quiet space rather than sharing a busy salon with other dogs.

Where are you located?

We're a home-based salon at 514 14 Ave NE in Renfrew, Calgary. You'll get the details you need when you book.

How much does a groom cost?

Pricing depends on your dog's size, coat and the service — call (403) 555-0106 for a quote.

What should I tell you when I call?

Your dog's breed, age and size, the condition of their coat (including any matting), how they've done with grooming before, and any health, skin or behaviour concerns. That helps us talk through the right service and a quote.

How should I prepare my dog for the appointment?

A short walk beforehand so they've had a chance to go to the bathroom is a good idea, and let us know about anything that's changed since you booked — a new skin issue, sore ears or a recent vet visit. For anything specific to your dog, ask when you call (403) 555-0106.

What are your hours?

Grooming is by appointment. Call for hours and availability.

My dog is anxious. Is this a good fit?

Many owners look for a quieter, one-on-one setting for exactly this reason. Let us know about your dog's needs when you call so we can talk about how best to approach their groom.
