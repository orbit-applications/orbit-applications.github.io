Questions

Good to know.

How much does a haircut cost?

Pricing depends on the service — a cut, a shave or the two together. Call (403) 555-0105 and the shop will let you know.

Do I need an appointment?

The simplest way to make sure there's a chair ready for you is to call ahead at (403) 555-0105 and ask about availability.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM. For anything outside those hours, call the shop to confirm.

How long does a visit take?

It depends on what you're having done — a line-up is quicker than a cut with a hot towel shave. Ask when you call and the shop can give you an idea.

What should I ask for?

Not sure? Bring a photo, or just describe how you like to wear it and how often you get it cut. Your barber can suggest what will suit.
