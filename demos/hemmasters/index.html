How much does hemming cost?
Jeans and pants hems C$20, dress pants C$22, zippers C$18, mends from C$15. Rush adds C$10 per garment. Call (403) 555-0145 for a quote.
Do I need an appointment?
You will find us at 1205 16 Ave NW in Calgary. Walk ins are welcome — call ahead if you are in a rush.
How long will it take?
It depends on the job and how busy we are. Ask about turnaround when you call and we'll give you an idea.
Can you fix leather or denim?
Yes — denim, leather and heavy fabrics are all on the bench. Describe the garment when you call.
Can you shorten sleeves?
Yes — jacket and shirt sleeves are a regular job. Call and tell us what you have.
Can I bring several garments?
Absolutely. List each piece when you call (403) 555-0145 so we can talk through pricing and timing.
