FAQ

Good questions.

Quick answers — or ask the barista next time you’re in.

Do you offer coffee subscriptions?+
Yes — a fresh bag every week or two, and subscriptions save 15%. Ask in store or call (403) 555-0133.
Can you grind beans for my brewer?+
Of course — tell us how you brew at home (pour over, French press, espresso) and we’ll grind to match.
Is there wifi? Can I work here?+
Yes — free wifi and a few laptop friendly tables. Mornings are the quietest time to settle in.
When is roast day?+
Every Thursday morning. Nothing on the shelf is ever more than a week off roast.
Do you host tastings or cuppings?+
Now and then — ask in store or call to hear what is coming up.
What beans are on espresso this week?+
A rotating single origin — ask the barista or call and we will tell you what is dialed in.
