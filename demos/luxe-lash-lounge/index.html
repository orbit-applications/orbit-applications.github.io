How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean, makeup free lashes. Avoid caffeine right before if you can — it makes eyes fluttery, and stillness gives the best retention.

How long does a full set take?

90 minutes to 2 hours depending on the set. We never rush — the time is part of the experience.

What is a keratin lash lift?

A nourishing treatment that curls your natural lashes upward, finished with tint. No extensions, no maintenance — lasts 6–8 weeks.

Do you do fills from other studios?

Yes, after a quick assessment. Call (403) 555-0127 and we'll tell you honestly whether a fill or fresh set is better.
