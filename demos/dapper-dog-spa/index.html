Good to Know

Questions, answered.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on your dog's size and the package — The Tidy up starts at C$55, The Full Dapper at C$95, and The Gentleman at C$150 for small dogs. Call (403) 555-0141 for a quote for your dog.

Do you take nervous dogs?

Yes. Appointments are one to one with no cages, and nervous dogs get patience, praise and plenty of breaks. Tell us about your dog when you book so we can plan the gentlest groom.

How far ahead should I book?

We usually book about a week out. Call or text and we'll find the next appointment that suits you.

Do you take walk ins?

Walk ins are welcome for nail trims only — all full grooms are by appointment so every dog gets the full 90 minutes.

What should I bring to the appointment?

Just your dog and their leash. If your dog has sensitive skin or a shampoo your vet recommended, bring that along and tell us when you call.

What if my dog needs a break during the groom?

Breaks are part of the groom here. Nervous dogs get patience, praise and time to settle, and we will call you if we think a shorter session suits them better.
