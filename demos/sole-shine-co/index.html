Deep Clean — C$35

Uppers, midsoles, outsoles and laces worked over by hand with material-safe products. C$35 a pair. The everyday reset for daily wearers, runners and gym shoes. When you call, tell us the brand, model and the spots that bother you most.

Sole Unyellowing — C$45

Yellowed or grey midsoles treated back toward bright. C$45 a pair. Oxidation builds up with age and sun — how far a sole comes back depends on the rubber and how long it has been yellowing. Describe your pair when you call and ask what is realistic.

Call to book

Suede & Nubuck Care — C$40

A gentle, nap-safe clean for suede, nubuck and knit — C$40 a pair. These materials stain and flatten easily, so they get a different method than leather or canvas. Mention water spots, salt or dirt when you call.

Restoration & Repaint — from C$95

For pairs that need more than a clean: scuff touch-ups, crease softening and repaint work on worn areas, from C$95. Best for older favourites and retros with faded or worn-through colour. Describe the damage when you call and we will talk through what is possible.

Odour & Salt Treatment — C$25

Insoles freshened, odour neutralized and winter salt lines lifted — C$25 a pair. A Calgary-winter essential after a slushy week. Ask about it on its own or added to a Deep Clean.

Protection Coat — C$20

A protective finish after cleaning to help your pairs resist water, salt and stains — C$20 a pair. Smart right after a deep clean, especially for suede and light-coloured shoes. Ask about it when you drop off.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? The fastest answer is a quick phone call during opening hours.

How much does it cost?

Deep Clean C$35, Sole Unyellowing C$45, Suede & Nubuck C$40, Protection C$20, Odour & Salt C$25, and Restoration & Repaint from C$95 — all per pair. Multi-pair drop-offs are worth asking about. Call (403) 555-0142 for an exact quote.

How long will it take?

A standard clean is usually back to you in a few days. Restoration takes longer. Ask about turnaround when you call and we will give you an idea.

Do I need to drop them off?

Yes — bring your pair to 412 11 Ave SW in the Beltline. Call ahead so we can set a drop-off time for you.

Can you save really beat-up shoes?

Sometimes! Describe the damage — stains, wear, yellowing — when you call and we will be straight with you about what is realistic for your pair.

What should I mention when I call?

The brand and model, the materials, and what bothers you most — stains, yellowing, odour or worn colour. A photo of the damage helps too.

Do you do winter salt stains?

Yes — salt stain removal is one of the things to ask about. Results depend on the material and how long the salt sat, so describe the shoe when you call — and the sooner after a slushy day, the better.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM, at 412 11 Ave SW, Calgary. Call ahead to book a drop-off time.
