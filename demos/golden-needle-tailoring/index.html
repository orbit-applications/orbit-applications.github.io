The difference fit makes

Good clothes deserve a good fit.

A jacket that sits cleanly across the shoulders, trousers that break just right, a shirt that doesn't billow at the waist. Small adjustments make the clothes in your wardrobe look like they were made for you.

For downtown professionals, being in Intact Place on the +15 means a fitting can fit around the workday — call to arrange a time.

Services

Alterations & repairs

Every garment and every body is different, so each job is quoted individually. Bring the piece in and we'll talk through what it needs.

No. I

Suit Alterations

Jacket sleeves shortened or lengthened, sides taken in, and trousers adjusted so the whole suit reads as one clean line. Ideal for a new off-the-rack suit or one that no longer fits quite the way it used to. Bring the shirt and shoes you'll wear with it to the fitting, and ask what can be adjusted when you call.

Call to enquire
No. II

Trouser Hemming

Hems set to the shoe you'll wear and the break you prefer, plus waist and seat adjustments for a comfortable fit. Whether you like no break, a slight break or a fuller one — with or without cuffs — it's set while you're wearing them. Bring the shoes you'll wear most often so the length comes out right.

Call to enquire
No. III

Shirts & Blouses

Shirts tapered through the body, sleeves shortened and collars or cuffs mended for a sharper look under a jacket. Especially useful for shirts that fit well in the collar and shoulders but feel boxy through the body. Wear or bring the shirt to your fitting so it can be pinned on you.

Call to enquire
No. IV

Jackets & Coats

Sleeve lengths, body shaping, linings and buttons on blazers, sport coats and outerwear. Shoulders are the hardest part of a jacket to change, so they matter most when buying — many other areas can be refined. Call to describe the piece and ask what's possible, from a blazer to a winter coat.

Call to enquire
No. V

Dresses & Formalwear

Hems, straps, waists and fit adjustments on dresses, skirts and occasion wear, so you're comfortable on the day. If it's for a wedding, gala or other event, mention the date when you call so timing can be planned. Bring the shoes and undergarments you'll wear for the most accurate fitting.

Call to enquire
No. VI

Repairs

Zippers replaced, seams re-sewn, tears and linings mended and buttons reattached — keeping good garments in service. Describe the damage when you call — a stuck zipper, a split seam or a torn lining — and ask what's possible. If the garment came with spare buttons, bring them along.

Questions

Before your fitting

A few common questions. For anything about your garment specifically, a quick call is best.

(403) 555-0104
How much do alterations cost?
It depends on the garment and the work involved. Call (403) 555-0104 for a quote.
Where exactly are you?
Suite 202, 321 6 Ave SW, in Intact Place — on the downtown +15 network. If you're unsure of the best way up, call and ask.
How long will my alteration take?
Timing depends on the job and the current workload. Ask about turnaround when you call or drop off, especially if you have an event date in mind.
Should I bring anything to my fitting?
It helps to bring the shoes you'll wear with trousers or a dress, and to wear the kind of shirt you'd normally pair with a jacket.
Do you do repairs as well as alterations?
Yes — call to describe the repair, whether it's a zipper, a seam, a lining or a tear, and we'll let you know what's possible.
Do you alter womenswear as well as menswear?
Yes — alterations are offered for both menswear and womenswear, from suits, trousers and shirts to dresses, skirts and blouses. Call (403) 555-0104 to describe your piece.
Can you alter something I just bought off the rack?
Call and describe the piece and what doesn't fit. Hems, sleeves and waists are common adjustments, while changes through the shoulders are harder — so if you're unsure, it's worth asking before the store's return window closes.
What are your hours?
Please call (403) 555-0104 for hours and availability.
