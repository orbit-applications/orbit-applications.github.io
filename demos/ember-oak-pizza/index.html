The oven

Built for fire.

Our oven burns Alberta oak and nothing else — no gas, no shortcuts. At 900°F the crust puffs, chars and blisters in about ninety seconds, the way pizza was meant to be made.

The dough ferments for 48 hours for flavor and chew. The tomatoes come from San Marzano. The fire does the rest.

Good to know

Before you come hungry.

Do you take reservations?+
We’re mostly walk in — call (403) 555-0121 ahead for parties of six or more and we’ll hold a table.
Do you deliver?+
Takeout, yes — call ahead and your pie hits the oven when you walk in. Delivery isn’t available yet.
Any gluten free or vegan pies?+
Ask when you call — we usually have a gluten free base and vegan mozzarella on request.
How long does takeout take?+
About 15 minutes from call to box. Fridays and Saturdays run a little longer — call early.
Do you cater events?+
Our oven can handle big orders with notice — call a week ahead for weddings and parties.
Is the dining room kid friendly?+
Yes — high chairs and a kids favourite Margherita. Come early on weekends for the quietest tables.
