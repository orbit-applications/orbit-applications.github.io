The oven

Built for fire.

Our oven burns Alberta oak and nothing else — no gas, no shortcuts. At 900°F the crust puffs, chars and blisters in about ninety seconds, the way pizza was meant to be made.

The dough ferments for 48 hours for flavor and chew. The tomatoes come from San Marzano. The fire does the rest.
