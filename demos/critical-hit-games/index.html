Questions

Asked at the counter every week

Anything else? Call or text (403) 555-0131 and we will check for you.

Do I need experience to play?

No. Your first game is free and a staff member teaches you the rules at the table, one turn at a time.

Can I bring my own army?

Yes. Painted or unpainted, any army is welcome at open play and at the monthly tournament.

Do you buy used miniatures?

We take trade ins for store credit. Bring them to the counter and we will price them with you on the spot.

What if a box is out of stock?

We order every week. Leave your name and number and we will call you the day it arrives.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount. They work in store on anything we carry, paints and tournament entries included.

Can I book a table for my group?

Yes. Use the booking form, pick a night, and the table is held under your name. Tables are free with any purchase in store.
