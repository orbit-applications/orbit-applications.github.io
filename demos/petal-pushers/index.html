Good to know

Questions? Answered

How fast is same day delivery?

Order by 1 PM and we deliver anywhere in Calgary the same day, usually within a few hours. Delivery is C$12 flat, free over C$75.

Can I add a card message?

Yes — every bouquet includes a free hand written card. Just tell us your message when you call (403) 555-0138.

How long will my flowers last?

Most bouquets last 7–10 days with fresh water and a trim every couple of days. Every order comes with care tips, and we back it with our 7 day freshness promise.

Do you do weddings and events?

Absolutely — bridal bouquets, centrepieces and full event florals. Call at least a few weeks ahead for weddings so we can plan your date together.

Do you deliver outside Calgary?

We deliver across Calgary and nearby towns like Cochrane and Okotoks. Call for a quote outside the city.

Can I order for today?

Yes, order by 1 pm for same day delivery. After 1 pm we will book the next morning.
