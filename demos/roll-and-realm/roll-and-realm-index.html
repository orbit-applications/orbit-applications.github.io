Questions

Good to know before you come

Do we need to know the rules?

No. Every game night starts with a staff member teaching the rules, and we stay close by for the first few turns.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Friday family night is built for kids, and our kids section is the biggest shelf in the store.

Can we try a game before buying it?

Yes. Pick anything from the demo library and play it at a table. If it is not a hit, put it back.

Can I return a game?

Unopened games can be returned within 30 days with a receipt. Opened games can be exchanged if every piece is there.

Do you help with gifts?

Yes. Tell us who it is for, their age, and what they already love. We will shortlist games they will actually play.

Can I book a table for a birthday or big group?

Yes. Book through the form and choose 7 or more players. We will text you to plan seating.
