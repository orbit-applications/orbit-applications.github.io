The pampering menu

Everything your pup needs to look & feel great

Every dog and every coat is a little different, so every groom is too. Tell us about your dog and we'll help you pick what fits.

Bath and blow dry

A warm, sudsy bath with a good rinse, then a fluff dry that leaves the coat soft, clean and smelling fresh. A good pick for short coated dogs or as a freshen up between haircuts. If your dog has sensitive skin or allergies, mention it when you call.

Call to book

Haircut and full groom

Bath, brush out and a tidy trim or full haircut, styled to suit your dog's coat and the look you're after. Tell us how short you'd like it, or bring a photo of a style you like. When you call, mention your dog's breed, size and roughly when they were last groomed.

Call to book

Brush out and deshedding

Loose undercoat and tangles worked out gently so double coated and fluffy breeds feel lighter and shed less around the house. Especially handy for huskies, shepherds, retrievers and other thick coated breeds when they're blowing their coat. Let us know about any tangles or mats when you book.

Call to book

Nail trims and paw tidy

Nails clipped to a comfortable length and the fur between the pads tidied up for better grip on slippery floors. It's a quick service that's easy to fit in between full grooms. If your dog is touchy about having their paws handled, give us a heads up when you call.

Call to book

Puppy's first groom

A short, gentle intro to the tub, the dryer and the brush — so your puppy learns that grooming day is a good day. Calm, positive early visits help make future grooms easier for everyone. Ask when you call about when to bring your puppy in and how to prepare them.

Call to book

Face, feet and tidy ups

In between full grooms? A quick tidy around the eyes, ears, feet and sanitary areas keeps your dog comfortable and neat. Handy for doodles, poodles, Shih Tzus and other breeds whose fur grows over the eyes and between the toes. Tell us which areas need attention when you call.

How much does a groom cost?

Pricing depends on your dog's size, coat type and condition, and the service you choose. Call (403) 555-0108 for a quote.

How long will my dog be there?

It varies with the dog and the service — a nail trim is much quicker than a full haircut. Ask about timing when you book and we'll let you know what to expect.

How often should my dog be groomed?

It depends on the breed and coat. Some dogs do well with an occasional bath and brush, while long or curly coats usually need more regular trims. Give us a call and we can suggest a routine for your dog.

My dog is nervous. Can you still groom them?

Please mention it when you call. Telling us about your dog's personality, any sensitivities or health concerns ahead of time helps us plan the gentlest groom we can.

What should I mention when I call?

Your dog's breed, size and age, what kind of coat they have, when they were last groomed and the service you're thinking of. It also helps to mention anything about their temperament, skin or health so we can plan the right groom.

My dog's coat is tangled or matted. What should I do?

Mention it when you call, along with roughly how long it's been since the last groom. Light tangles can often be brushed out, but heavier matting sometimes needs a different approach for your dog's comfort, so it's best to talk it through over the phone first.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM, at 838 20 Ave NW, Calgary. Call ahead to book a time.
