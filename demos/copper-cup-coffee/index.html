FAQ

Quick sips.

Do you have wifi?+
Yes — fast, free, no password hoops. Plenty of outlets along the window bar for laptops.
Is there a charge for oat milk?+
Never. Oat, almond and soy are always free here.
Can I bring my dog?+
Dogs are welcome on the patio, and we keep treats behind the counter. Well behaved pups only, please.
Do you sell beans to take home?+
Yes — 340g bags of our house espresso and rotating single origins, $19. Grind it however you brew.
Where are you located?+
5520 4 St NW, Calgary — on the corner, look for the copper awning.
What are your hours?+
Monday to Friday 6:30 AM–6 PM, Saturday 7 AM–6 PM, Sunday 8 AM–4 PM.
