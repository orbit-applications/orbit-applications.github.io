How far in advance should we book?

Weddings: 6–12 months, especially for summer Saturdays. Family sessions: 2–4 weeks is usually plenty.

Do you travel?

Happily — the mountains are my second studio. Travel within 100 km is included; beyond that we just talk it through.

How many photos do we get?

Family: 40+. Elopement: 150+. Wedding: 400+. Every image is hand-edited in my signature warm style.

What if it rains?

We embrace it — some of my favourite galleries were shot in the rain. We'll also have a backup plan, always.

Can we meet first?

Please! A coffee or video call before booking is part of the process — we should click before I photograph your biggest day.
