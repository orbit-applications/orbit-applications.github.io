Take a look inside

Designed for kids, built for parents. Here's what FamQuest looks like.

Built for kids. Designed for parents.

⚡

Instant stars

Check off a chore, get stars immediately. No waiting for approval — the dopamine hits right away.

🎁

Prize shelf

An arcade-style shelf stocked by parents. Save up stars, pick your reward, watch the progress bar fill.

🔥

Streaks & levels

Daily streaks, XP levels, and Nova the mascot cheering you on. Streak freezes forgive bad days.

🎴

Weekly surprises

Pick a face-down card each week for a surprise reward. Every card is parent-chosen — no gambling, just fun.

🦊

Dress up Nova

Customize your fox companion with colors and hats. Nova evolves as you level up.

🏆

Sibling leaderboards

Friendly weekly races with siblings. Winner gets a crown and bonus stars — no shaming, just fun.

📋

Activity log

Every star event in real time. If a kid fibbed, tap "Take back" — balance clamps at zero, kid gets a kind note.

🏪

Shelf manager

Stock prizes with photos and prices. Set star values, manage stock, create mystery prizes.

💰

Allowances

Weekly star allowances per kid, on autopilot. Pause anytime. Real money stays in your bank — stars are just stars.

📊

Insights

See who's doing what. Stars per kid, completion rates, 7-day trends, and gentle tips from Nova.

💬

Family chat

Family-only messaging. No friends, no strangers, no discovery — just your household.

🔒

Safe by design

No ads, no tracking, no real money in the app. Kid profiles with PIN or Face ID. You control everything.

1

3

Prizes get claimed

Hi, I'm Nash 👋

I build apps, and I kept watching families struggle with the same thing: getting kids to do chores without it turning into a negotiation every single time.

Sticker charts work for a week. Allowance apps feel like banking software. Nothing made chores actually fun.

So I'm building FamQuest — the app I wish existed. Chores become quests, effort becomes stars, and stars become real rewards. Kids stay motivated, parents stay sane.

— Nash, Orbit Applications
