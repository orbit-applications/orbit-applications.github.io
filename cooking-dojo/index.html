Start with the skill every beginner gets wrong: knife skills

Most beginner cooking lessons jump straight to recipes — then you spend the whole time fighting your knife. Cooking Dojo's Foundations path starts where it should: how to hold a chef's knife, the claw grip that keeps your fingers safe, and the three cuts (dice, mince, julienne) behind 80% of home cooking. Each lesson takes minutes, and a quiz every fifth lesson locks it in.

Beginner cooking questions, answered

I'm a total beginner. Where do I even start learning to cook?

Start with knife skills and heat control — the two foundations every recipe assumes you know. Cooking Dojo's Foundations path teaches both in bite-sized daily lessons, then unlocks real recipes that use them.

How is Cooking Dojo different from recipe apps?

Recipe apps assume you can already cook. Cooking Dojo teaches one skill at a time, in order, with quizzes and streaks — so a total beginner builds real technique instead of just following steps.

Is Cooking Dojo free?

Yes — the core app is free with no ads. Optional Dojo Gold and Platinum subscriptions add a bigger energy tank, exclusive icons and avatar items.

Is there an Android version?

iOS only for now — Cooking Dojo is made by a one-person indie team nailing one platform first. Android is on the roadmap if there's enough demand.

How long are the lessons?

Each lesson takes just a few minutes — designed as a daily habit, with one gentle reminder a day and never spam.
