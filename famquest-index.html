Everything family life needs, together.

FamQuest combines chores, allowances, school wins, healthier screen habits, rewards, and safe communication in one place.

Chores become quests—and stars

Assign one-time or repeating chores and choose the reward. Stars arrive instantly when a chore is checked off, every event appears in the parent Activity Log, and parents can take stars back if a kid wasn’t honest.

A star economy that makes sense

You control the value. Set an exchange rate—like a $20 weekly allowance equaling 10 stars—and FamQuest converts a prize’s cash price into stars as you add it. Kids learn that rewards have real value without the math becoming another chore.

$20 weekly value→10 stars→$10 prize = 5 stars

Weekly allowance, automated

Choose a cash amount and schedule once. The matching stars land in each kid’s balance automatically every week.

Weekly Surprise Pick

Stock 2–5 surprise options for each kid. On surprise day, they choose one face-down card and get a big reveal.

Kids suggest their own prizes

“I want this!” sends a photo, reason, and request to the parent Activity Log. Approve it and set the star price—or decline it with a note.

School wins—with parent verification

Kids log a mark, parents verify it against the school website, and only then are bonus stars awarded. No automatic school connection and no unverified rewards.

Family chat, kept inside the family

Kids can message parents and siblings from their profile. Every conversation stays within the family, and parents can see every message.

Quest approved—nice job!
Can we do movie night Friday?

Habits and routines

Build repeatable morning, bedtime, homework, and self-care routines. A simple daily check-in keeps progress visible without extra nagging.

Screen-time challenges

Turn healthier limits into a goal: “Stay under two hours today for +3 stars.” Parents choose the target and reward.

Streaks and sibling leaderboards

Daily streaks unlock bonus stars, while a fresh weekly family leaderboard adds friendly competition without keeping permanent scores.

Kid-safe by design

No ads—ever. Kids don’t create accounts or share personal sign-in details; they use profiles inside the parent-managed family. Optional profile PINs keep siblings out of one another’s balances, and parents remain in control of approvals, rewards, and family messages.

  • Parent account with invite codes for the other adults in the family
  • Kid profiles instead of separate accounts
  • No ads, no public feed, and no hidden messages

At-a-glance widgets

See today’s quests, current star balance, and the next family goal from the home screen—without opening the app first.
